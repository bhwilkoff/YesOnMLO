# "Now it's our turn": throwback research

Follow-up to the Arapahoe 1993 reel (~10K FB views). Researched 2026-10-20.
Every YouTube link below was re-checked the same day (title + uploader confirmed).

## The facts that make the message true

Use these as written; each has a source.

- "Voters have approved all six of the district's previous requested bonds
  and mill levies, dating back to 1988." (Littleton Independent, 2018,
  https://www.coloradocommunitymedia.com/archives/littleton/news/article_e7113797-cc73-5eb1-adaf-b65f4043f597.html)
- Overrides passed in 1988, 1997, 2004 and 2010; the 1988 and later
  overrides are still part of how LPS is funded today. (LPS 2024-25 Adopted
  Budget, Informational Section pp. 180, 187, via Wayback)
- 2002: $85.4M bond, the district's largest until 2018 (same 2018 article).
  It paid for roofs, security and fire systems at LHS, Arapahoe, Ames, Runyon,
  Twain, Whitman, and Arapahoe's all-weather track and tennis courts (Littleton
  Museum high/elementary school history pages).
- 2018: the bond was on the ballot as **Bond 4A** (same article) and passed
  56.83% (CAMPAIGN-BRIEF). Same ballot number as this year.
- 1950s-60s: "a series of increasingly large bond issues were approved by
  voters to finance all the new construction" (12 elementaries, 3 junior
  highs, Littleton High School, Arapahoe). History Colorado, *Historic Context of
  Littleton 1949-1967*, p. 20:
  https://www.historycolorado.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/2018/si_postwwii_littleton_1949-1967.pdf
- No failed LPS school measure found anywhere.

Opening years (Littleton Museum): LHS building 1956, Arapahoe 1964, Heritage
1972, Euclid 1959, Newton 1962, Goddard 1968, Powell 1981, Centennial and
Highland 1958, Hopkins 1962, Field 1963, Sandburg 1966-67 (60 this year),
Runyon 1969, Twain 1971, Wilder 1974, Lenski 1979. Littleton Academy opened
Sept 3, 1996 (30 this year).

Do NOT say: "voters built Heritage" (no bond vote tied to it yet); a
"1960 bond failed" (that was the parks district, not LPS); anything about a
1995 bond (projects real, election unverified).

## Yearbook scans, pulled 2026-10-22

archive.org `classmates-yearbook-*` items (Classmates.com scans). Most "Littleton
High School" items are NH, MA or NC; most "Heritage" ones are VA, CA or IN. The
Littleton, CO ones (per each item's description): Arapahoe 1973 (32916);
Heritage 1976 (41917), 1977 (24595), 1980 (24919), 1981 (9005); LHS 1957
(11589), 1963 (25809), 1964 (27759), 1981 (18699), 1982 (12505), 1984 (11024).
PDFs, contact sheets and text layers are in `source-files/yearbook-scans/`.

Best pages: supporting staff. LHS 1957 p19 (cafeteria + custodians, named);
LHS 1964 p39 ("The efficiency of our school depends upon the cooks, headed by
Mrs. Oman, the custodians, by Mr. Wedow and the bus drivers by Mr. Davidson");
AHS 1973 p213; HHS 1977 p209 (65-cent lunch, ~400 a day); HHS 1980 pp152-153
(up to 700 a day, 75 lbs of beans). LHS 1957 p58: the new building.
Rights: no one has given permission. Yearbook copyright likely sits with LPS or
the student publication; posting small excerpts is common practice, but it's Ben's call.
