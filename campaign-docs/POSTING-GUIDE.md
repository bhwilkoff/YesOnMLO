# @citizens4lps launch kit

Built 2026-09-23. Every file in this folder is sized for Instagram:
1080×1350 for feed, 1080×1920 for Stories and Reels.

## Why it's built this way

- **The photos stay as they came off the phone.** No filters, no
  retouching, no AI edits. Instagram adds an "AI info" label to
  anything its tools detect as AI-edited, and people scroll past
  polished work. Landscape shots sit on plain white or black, the way
  the No Crop apps do it.
- **The text copies Instagram's own Classic style**, black on a white
  highlight, so it looks like it was typed in the app. Every story
  also has a `-CLEAN` version with no text, if you'd rather type it
  yourself in the app.
- **A new account can only grow through reach to people who don't
  follow it yet.** Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, has named three
  signals that matter most: watch time, sends per reach, and likes
  per reach. For reaching non-followers, sends (people DMing a post to
  a friend) count the most. So the plan is:
  - a short Reel that loops, since that's what reaches non-followers
  - Chance, because dog posts get sent to friends
  - **Collab posts.** This is the biggest lever for an account at zero
    followers. A Collab post shows up in the feed of everyone who
    follows the collaborator's account too.
- Mosseri suggests about 2 Reels and 3–5 feed posts a week.

## Before the first post

- **Name field:** `Citizens for LPS | Yes on 4A`. The name field is
  searchable, the handle is only partly searchable.
- **Bio:**
  `Neighbors for Yes on 4A, the LPS mill levy override. Littleton & Centennial. Ballots mail Oct 2. Vote by Nov 3.`
- **Link:** `citizensforlps.org`. The root domain works.
  **Subpages don't.** `citizensforlps.org/volunteer` returns a 404,
  because the domain only forwards its root to NationBuilder. Any
  link sticker that goes deeper than the root has to use
  `citizensforlps.nationbuilder.com/...`.
- **Profile photo:** the stacked logo from `branding/yes-on-4a/`.
- **Account type:** if you pick Business, the Reels music library
  shrinks to the royalty-free collection. Creator keeps the full
  library. Either one works for the Reel below.
- On the Facebook page, post "We're on Instagram now: @citizens4lps."
  That page has 574 followers who already care.

## Not used, and why

- **The CBS video itself.** It's a monitoring-service copy stamped
  "For Internal Use Only," and CBS owns it. Re-uploading it risks a
  takedown on an account that's one day old. Link to it instead (Story
  2). The transcript is in `cbs-colorado-transcript.md`. CBS says
  "all district staff will get a 2% pay raise." The district's figure
  is a 2% **average**. Use ours.
- **The park kickoff photo.** Candidate signs for Joan Lopez and Ilana
  Spiegel are in the frame. 4A needs voters from both parties, and a
  slate photo on the committee's account says otherwise. It also shows
  about fifteen identifiable people with no releases.
