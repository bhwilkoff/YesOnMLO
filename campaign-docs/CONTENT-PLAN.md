Every clip has captions burned in, because most people watch with the
sound off. The CBS clips crop out the station's news ticker (it was
running unrelated national headlines) and the "For Internal Use Only"
stamp. They keep the CBS Colorado logo, since CBS gets the credit, and
end on a 3-second card: "Yes on 4A, the LPS mill levy override.
Ballots mail Oct 2. citizensforlps.org."

**The Toddcast clips have no campaign end card.** The ask goes in our
caption, and the caption names the source: Dr. Lambert, the
district's podcast. So Lambert never looks like he's the one saying
"vote yes." And we don't tag LPS accounts on these or make them a
Collab with one.

## The rhythm

- **Instagram:** a feed post or Reel most days this stretch, plus
  Stories daily. Mosseri's number for growth is 2 Reels and 3–5 posts
  a week, and the forum week plus ballot day justify more.
- **Facebook:** one post a day. That audience skews older and
  homeowner-heavy, so it gets the longer explainers (the full CBS
  story, the property-tax clip) first.
- **Same files on both platforms.** Upload natively to each. Don't
  share a post from one into the other. Native video gets several
  times the reach of a shared link.
