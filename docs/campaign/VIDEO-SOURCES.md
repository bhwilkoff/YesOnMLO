# Where the video and archival material came from

Every outside source we pulled from for campaign videos and throwback posts, in one
place. Links were checked on November 5, 2026. Most of this material is not ours, so
this page points to it rather than copying it.

**A link here isn't permission to use something.** Read the rights notes before you
reuse anything, and see `MEDIA-PLAYBOOK.md`.
