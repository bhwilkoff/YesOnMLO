# Colorado school tax measures, November 2026

A snapshot of every school question we could find on Colorado ballots in 2026, made to
see how 4A, the LPS mill levy override, did against its neighbors.

**These numbers are unofficial and incomplete.** They're from county results as of
midday November 5, 2026, with counting still under way in the big counties. Use this for
the shape of the year, and check certified results for anything that matters. Jeffco 5A
in particular was close enough to change.

## What it showed

- **37 school questions, 23 ahead.** Some of the smallest may not be tax measures.
- **LPS at 55.1%** was about 5.5 points ahead of Jeffco 5A and nearly 10 ahead of Douglas
  County. Denver and Westminster did better, but they're different electorates, and
  Westminster's ask was much smaller.
- **It was LPS's lowest in recent memory:** the four measures from 2010 to 2020 ran from
  56.83% to 60.34%. The brief predicted the headwinds: property-tax fatigue and many
  districts asking at once.
- **Size didn't settle it.** Big asks passed in Denver and failed in Douglas County, and
  small ones went both ways.

## How to rebuild this

The Secretary of State's results feed lists every county's election ID. Each county's
`summary.json` has its contests, so you can scan all 64 for school measures. Add a
district's counties together, since many cross county lines. Dollar amounts came from the
[Keating Research tracker](https://keatingresearch.com/local-ballot-measures/colorado-2026/)
and news coverage.
