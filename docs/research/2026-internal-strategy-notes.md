# Internal strategy notes, August 2026

These were private during the campaign and are published now that it's over (Decision 063).
They're the notes as they stood in late August, before the tagline was picked. The final
tagline was "Invest for a strong LPS." See `docs/campaign/RETROSPECTIVE.md` for how it went.

## Branding (steering committee, August 26, 2026)

- **Kept:** the mortar-board icon and the "Yes on 4..." logo from the prior campaign.
- **Replaced:** the "Keep our schools strong" tagline.
- **Contenders then:** Local Funding for LPS Kids, Investing in the Future of LPS,
  Supporting the Best for LPS.
- **What a tagline had to do:**
  - Not be generic. Prop NN was on the same ballot and many districts were running
    overrides, so it had to be clearly ours.
  - Reach swing voters. "Local funding" and "money that stays in LPS" were landing with
    more conservative-leaning voters.
  - Include "LPS."
  - Read at driving speed on a yard sign.
  - Avoid "Littleton," so we didn't alienate Centennial voters.

## Audiences

- **Persuasion target:** swing and conservative-leaning suburban voters. Lead with local
  control, dollars staying in LPS classrooms, accountability and teacher retention, not
  abstract "fund schools" language.
- **Base:** parents, staff and education-first voters. Mobilize them. Don't re-persuade.
- **Watch:** seniors and homeowners without kids in school. Tax clarity and property values
  matter most here.
