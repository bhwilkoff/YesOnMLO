# Why Colorado's count took so long in 2026

Researched November 4, 2026, while we waited on results. Nothing we found suggested a
problem with the count. We found no official explanation for Arapahoe County's pace in
particular, so the Arapahoe parts are partly inference, and they're marked.

## What drove it

**A late wave of ballots.** Statewide returns went from 1,599,988 through November 1 to
2,671,424 through Election Day, so 40% of all ballots arrived in the last two days.
Arapahoe was at about 174,000 around Monday and reached 269,853 by Election Day night.
The next day's state report showed 275,291 received in Arapahoe, so that number kept
moving as ballots were logged.

**Early ballots go first.** Counties could start processing on October 19. That's why
Arapahoe's first drop had 187,231 ballots at 7:20 p.m. The state says ballots received on
Election Day will likely be reported later.

**Size.** In the June primary, officials said big counties take "several days, as is
typical given the number of ballots." November looked the same. Here is each county's vote
for Governor as a share of ballots received, on the evening of November 4:

| County | Counted |
|---|---|
| Boulder | 77% |
| Arapahoe | 78% |
| Denver | 81% |
| El Paso | 84% |
| Jefferson | 90% |
| Douglas | 94% |
| Mesa | 97% |

**A long ballot.** Arapahoe's results feed had 82 contests. That this added time is our
inference, not something an official said.

**Ballots that can't finish yet.** Voters had until November 12 to fix signature or ID
problems, and military and overseas ballots could arrive through then.

## Possible but unconfirmed

**The Issue 7A error.** About 262,000 Arapahoe voters were mailed the Front Range Passenger
Rail question though they live outside the district. A local report said this meant extra
steps. No source we found says it slowed other contests.

**A state software problem.** Gunnison County's clerk blamed a statewide glitch for
stalling signature checks. Nobody else we found confirmed it.

## Ruled out

**The election-night website crash.** It was real, but the state says it affected only
viewing results, not counting or uploads.
