# Fact-Check Audit — every claim on the site (2026-08-26)

All quotes pulled directly from the cited pages (fetched 2026-08-26)
or from official PDFs on county/state servers. This audit is the
provenance layer behind `js/data.js` (Decisions 054, 058): a claim
appears on the site only if it has a VERIFIED row here.

## 1. $10.6M deficit + the one-time measures — VERIFIED (with precision notes)

LPS Superintendent's letter, May 29, 2026
(https://www.littletonpublicschools.net/article/2950007):

> "That gap for next year in LPS is $10.6 million, a substantial cut
> with significant ramifications."

> "To date, we have reduced $1.1 million in central office staffing
> and another $2.8 million from district operating budgets through
> position eliminations, reduced hours, and department restructuring.
> We have also shifted certain expenses to the Ops-Tech fund, creating
> $5.4 million in general fund savings, and eliminated one-time
> expenditures to save an additional $500,000."

> "…a 2026–2027 budget that does not include a cost of living
> increase for employees. We are also recommending the Board approve
> a furlough day, which saves LPS an additional $800,000."

Aug 14, 2026 press release
(https://www.littletonpublicschools.net/article/3075947) confirms:
"…including an employee wage freeze and a scheduled districtwide
furlough day…"

Precision: the $5.4M is general-fund *savings from shifting expenses
to the Ops-Tech fund*, not cuts — don't sum it into a "cuts" figure.
Denver7 adds the furlough day is March 19 and the comp hold saves
~$2.5M.

## 5. Senior homestead exemption — VERIFIED

DPT (https://dpt.colorado.gov/senior-property-tax-exemption): 65+ on
Jan 1, owned/occupied 10+ consecutive years, exempts 50% of the first
$200,000 of actual value. Application window Jan 1 – Jul 15.
