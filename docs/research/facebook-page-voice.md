# How our Facebook page sounded, and what got a response

We read the Citizens for LPS Facebook page as its owner on September 9, 2026, before
posting anything. It had 574 followers and history from the 2020 "Yes on 4C" campaign. It
went quiet after February 2021. Doing this first is worth an hour for any page you inherit.

## The voice

- One or two sentences, almost never long.
- The ". . ." with spaces was the page's signature.
- Lots of exclamation points, and "&" for "and".
- Grateful first.
- "BREAKING NEWS:" for endorsements.
- Lowercase hashtags. We moved to `#YesOn4A` style, since capitals are easier for screen
  readers.

A typical post from 2020: "8:36 p.m. results . . .thank you for keeping our community and
schools strong."

## What drew a response

| Kind of post | Reactions |
|---|---|
| Election week, celebration, results | 20 to 74 |
| Yard sign logistics | about 20 |
| Link shares (news, letters, endorsements) | 5 to 14 |
| Off-season | 1 to 11 |

## What we did about it

Lead with a native image, keep posts short and warm, and put links in a comment or plain
text. Campaign-brochure writing has never sounded like this page, and the numbers say it
doesn't travel.
